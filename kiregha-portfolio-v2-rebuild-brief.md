## 1. Audit of the current public site

The current site has useful raw material, but its story is behind the work:

- The journey timeline stops at 2024, while the project cards already show 2026 work.
- The homepage describes the developer as a generic full-stack engineer interested in scalable platforms and AI, instead of explaining the specific systems being built.
- The skills section lists a very broad set of tools without showing which are used regularly, which are working knowledge, and which were academic exposure.
- The homepage displays 4+ years, 15+ projects, 25+ technologies and 3 certifications. Keep these only if each number can be substantiated and defined. Otherwise remove the counters.
- The current project descriptions are too short to show the problem, personal contribution, technical decisions, trade-offs or delivery status.
- Some descriptions make strong claims (for example, inventory prediction, behaviour analysis, early detection and AI-powered features) without explaining whether these are shipped features, prototypes or proposals.
- The URUTI Hub dates are inconsistent: the homepage says 2024–Present, while the public CV says June–August 2025. The internship logbook has also been described as beginning on 1 July 2025. Confirm the formal dates and engagement type before publishing one consistent version.
- The downloadable CV is six pages and should be brought into line with the website. Remove private residential detail and referees' personal contact details from the public copy.

Do not redesign the site by simply changing colours, adding animations or generating more generic copy. Make the evidence and the story the centre of the redesign.
